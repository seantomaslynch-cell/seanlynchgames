What the app itself collects

Nothing

Gridiron GM makes no network calls of its own. Your career (your team, roster, cap sheet, draft history, every season you've played) is a save file that lives only on your device. There is no account, no login, no server we operate, and no way for us to see your save even if we wanted to.

What the two SDKs collect

Third-party, optional, disclosed here

The app uses two outside services, only for the two things this kind of game reasonably needs: showing an optional rewarded advert, and processing an optional purchase. Neither can see your game.

Google AdMob
Powers the opt‑in "watch an advert for a token" button. Adverts are requested non‑personalized unless you allow tracking (see below). AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier to serve and measure that advert. Nothing plays automatically: every advert is a screen you chose to open.
RevenueCat
Processes optional in‑app purchases (token packs, Token Boost, Front Office Pass) through Apple's App Store. It sees a purchase receipt and an anonymous device identifier, no name, email, or payment details pass through Gridiron GM.

Advertising identifier

iOS App Tracking Transparency

When you first open the app, iOS asks whether Gridiron GM may track you (the App Tracking Transparency prompt). In Europe, the UK and Switzerland this comes right after Google's ad consent form. If you tap Allow, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to personalize and measure the optional reward adverts, which counts as tracking under Apple's rules. If you decline, adverts stay non‑personalized and the identifier is not shared. Either way the game plays exactly the same, and the "watch an advert" button works the same. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Children

Rated 4+ · general audience

Gridiron GM is rated for a general audience and is not directed at children. It contains no chat, no user‑generated content, and no way to contact another person through the app.
