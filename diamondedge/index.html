DiamondEdgeMatchups, trends and breakdowns in one app.

DiamondEdge is a sports analytics app. Get matchups, player trends and game breakdowns for the NFL, college football, NBA, WNBA and MLB, all in one app. It is free on iPhone and Android.

Free on iPhone and Android

What you get

  • Matchups for the games you follow.
  • Player trends to read at a glance.
  • Game breakdowns in one place.
  • Covers the NFL, college football, NBA, WNBA and MLB.
  • A Players screen with sport filters and a list of player prop lines.
