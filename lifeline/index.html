LifeLineOne life. Every choice is yours.

LifeLine is a life simulator. Live a whole life, make the choices that shape it, and see where it takes you. It is free on iPhone and Android.

Free on iPhone and Android

Screenshots

What you get

  • Live a whole life from start to finish.
  • Make the choices that shape it.
  • Keep an eye on your character profile and life stats.
  • Pick from a choice of actions, then press Age to move your life forward.
  • See where your choices take you.
