Gatecrash Army tips: grow a bigger army and beat the boss

Gatecrash Army is a free action runner for iPhone and Android. Drag left and right to steer, and your squad shoots on its own. These tips help you finish stages with a bigger army.

App StoreGoogle Play

Gates

  • Pick the gate that multiplies your squad the most.
  • Shoot a gate before you reach it to push its number higher.
  • Break barrels for bonus soldiers and firepower, and dodge saws and walls.

Weapons, officers and perks

  • Grab a shotgun, minigun, laser or rockets for a few seconds of extra firepower.
  • Pick an officer with a special ability, like an airstrike or a shield wall.
  • After every win, pick 1 of 3 perks. They stack until you lose, so a win streak gets stronger.

Bosses and the bonus stairs

  • A giant boss waits at the end of a stage.
  • Beat it and your squad charges up the bonus stairs, where every step multiplies your coins.
  • Every soldier you keep alive counts, so play safe near the end.

Modes

  • Chaos Mode gives every stage a random world and twist, and your squad carries over.
  • Gate Frenzy is pure gate math: grow a huge army, then crash it into a horde.
  • Boss Rush, Endless March and Zombie Horde round out the modes, each free to play every day.

Between runs

  • Build up the Outpost with the scrap you win.
  • Daily missions, a daily challenge, a daily spin and a free chest every few hours add up.
  • Nothing for sale makes your squad stronger. Purchases are looks, ad removal and extra ways to play. It plays offline.

Get Gatecrash Army

Gatecrash Army is free on iPhone and Android. See the Gatecrash Army page, the latest updates, or ask questions in the Discord.
