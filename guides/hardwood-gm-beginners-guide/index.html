Hardwood GM beginner's guide: run a basketball team

Hardwood GM is a free, text-based basketball general manager sim for iPhone and Android. You do not play the games. You run the team above them. Here is what to learn first.

App StoreGoogle Play

Spend your Action Points well

  • You get four Action Points a day, so you cannot talk to every player.
  • Every player has a personality, like an Alpha Dog who needs the ball or a Ring Chaser running out of years.
  • Praise the wrong player and it lands badly. Ignore a complaint and it can turn into a trade demand.

Trades

  • Rival GMs send real offers, answer your trade block and take a counter.
  • Ask what it would take to get any player in the league.
  • The trade machine weighs talent, age, upside and contract value, and will not let a pile of role players buy a superstar.
  • Three-team deals, sign-and-trades and cash are possible until the February deadline.

The draft is all season

  • Your scouts work the next class from opening night.
  • Use the combine and private workouts to learn who is a gym rat and who is a locker-room risk.
  • The lottery rewards a real rebuild.

Learn the cap

  • There is a salary cap, a luxury tax and aprons, and contracts rise each year.
  • Your own free agents hold cap room until you re-sign or renounce them.
  • Two-way deals, buyouts and a supermax for a star you drafted are all part of it.

Make the call

  • Watch three quarters, then make the coach's call for the fourth: ride the star, go small or tighten the perimeter.
  • The call is a matchup read, not a boost. You can also set tempo, a closing lineup and minutes caps.

Your career

  • Get fired and other teams call with offers. Win enough and teams come to you.
  • Careers are copied to iCloud Drive and can be backed up to a file. No account needed.
  • Ads are optional rewards, and no pack changes a rating, a contract or a result.

Get Hardwood GM

Hardwood GM is free on iPhone and Android. See the Hardwood GM page, the latest updates, or ask questions in the Discord.
