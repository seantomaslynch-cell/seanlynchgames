Gatecrash Army War Pass: tiers, XP, rewards and seasons

The War Pass is the season track in Gatecrash Army. You fill it with XP from your runs and daily tasks, and it pays out coins, scrap and looks for your squad. A season has 30 tiers and lasts 28 days. This guide explains how XP works, what each track pays, what the pass costs, and how to finish a season without wasting a run. For gates, bosses and the base, see the Gatecrash Army tips, the upgrades and bosses guide and the Outpost guide.

How the War Pass works

  • A season has 30 tiers. Each tier takes 400 XP, so tier 30 needs 12,000 XP in total.
  • There are two tracks. The free track is open to everyone. The premium track is a paid purchase, one per season.
  • You reach a tier by earning XP. Then you tap to claim its reward. A reward you have not claimed waits for you, so nothing is lost by claiming later.
  • You can claim a whole row at once with the claim all button. It pays every reward you can take on both tracks.
  • Money never buys power here. The premium track pays cosmetics only. It never pays coins, scrap, or anything that changes how a run plays.

How to earn War Pass XP

Every finished run pays XP, win or lose. A run pays a base of 6 XP. You add 1 XP for every 10 kills, up to 20 XP from kills. A win adds 12 XP. Each boss you beat adds 5 XP, and up to 4 bosses count, so bosses add at most 20 XP.

Endless March, Gate Frenzy and Chaos add 4 XP for each stage you reach, up to 20 XP. That makes a long run in those modes a good XP source even if you do not win.

A typical campaign win is worth about 40 XP. At that rate you need about 300 such wins for a full season from runs alone, which is why the daily tasks matter.

Daily XP

  • Daily login: 60 XP when you claim the login reward.
  • Daily mission: 60 XP each time you claim a mission reward.
  • Trophy: 40 XP when you claim a trophy reward.

Those three add up to 160 XP on a day you do all of them. Over the 28 days of a season, that is a floor of 4,480 XP before you play a single run.

What the free track pays

The free track pays something on every tier. Most tiers pay coins. Every third or so tier pays scrap, and the scrap amounts grow as you climb. Three tiers pay a free cosmetic: a hat at tier 6, a skin at tier 14 and a skin at tier 22. Tier 30 pays a big coin reward.

Season 1 pays 60 coins at tier 1 and 500 coins at tier 30. Over a full Season 1, the free track pays 3,520 coins and 270 scrap, plus its three looks. Later seasons pay a little more each time:

  • Season 1: 3,520 coins and 270 scrap. Tier 30 pays 500 coins.
  • Season 2: 3,740 coins and 295 scrap. Tier 30 pays 550 coins.
  • Season 3: 3,960 coins and 318 scrap. Tier 30 pays 600 coins.
  • Season 4: 4,095 coins and 329 scrap. Tier 30 pays 650 coins.
  • Season 5: 4,230 coins and 340 scrap. Tier 30 pays 700 coins.

The scrap goes straight into your Outpost, so a finished pass is also a few free building upgrades. See the Outpost guide for what scrap buys.

What the premium track pays

Premium is paid. You buy it once per season, and each season is its own purchase. The store shows the price on your device. The game also sells a bundle that covers the premium track of every season. Nothing in the premium track changes your squad's strength.

Each season's premium track has 13 rewards, all cosmetic. They sit on these tiers: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27, 29 and 30. Here is what a season's 13 rewards are made of:

  • 5 skins, at tiers 3, 7, 12, 21 and 30.
  • 4 hats, at tiers 1, 9, 18 and 27.
  • 3 tracer looks, at tiers 5, 15 and 24.
  • 1 victory effect, at tier 29.

Tier 30 is the headline premium prize of each season, a skin with the theme of that season. The premium track has 13 rewards across 30 tiers, so 17 premium tiers are empty and the row looks sparse in the middle. That is by design.

Buying late is fine

  • Buying premium mid season unlocks every premium tier you have already reached, so you do not lose progress.
  • If you buy a past season's pass after it ended, the game pays all of that season's premium looks at once.
  • You do not need premium to finish the free track. The two tracks fill from the same XP.

The five themed seasons

Each season has a name, a theme, and a campaign rule that changes how levels play. The rule is the same for every player. It is never tied to whether you bought the pass.

  • Season 1: the classic season. It has no special rule.
  • Season 2, Galaxy Raid: the Meteor Shower rule. Meteors fall from the sky, so dodge the red rings. A rocket crate shows up more often.
  • Season 3, Frozen Front: the Ice Field rule. Ice slows enemies down, and saws freeze into ice blocks. A freeze crate shows up more often.
  • Season 4, Jungle Strike: the Jungle Loot rule. You see more mystery gates and more treasure, and you need to watch for vine walls.
  • Season 5, Neon Nights: the Neon Night rule. It is a night level with more laser crates and a longer combo.

Learn the rule of the season you are in. The Frozen Front rule helps you fight, and the Meteor Shower rule asks you to move more. The tips guide covers steering and gate choices that work in any season.

The season clock

  • A season lasts 28 days. The clock starts when your season starts.
  • When the clock runs out, the game pays everything you can still claim, then moves you to the next season. The free track always pays. The premium track pays if you own it.
  • Your XP, tier and claims reset for the new season. Bonus crate XP carries over.
  • The days left show on the menu. On the last day it says Last day.
  • The game waits an hour after the clock ends before it rolls the season over. That protects you if your phone clock was wrong for a moment.

To finish in time, you need an average of about 430 XP a day. Daily tasks cover 160 of that. A few runs a day cover the rest.

Bonus crates after tier 30

XP past tier 30 is not thrown away. It fills bonus crates. Every 600 XP past the last tier gives one crate. Each crate pays 150 coins and 15 scrap, and it is free for everyone. The crates carry over into the next season, so a strong finish still pays off.

The XP boost

If you fall behind late in a season, the game can offer a rewarded video for a boost. The boost gives double War Pass XP for your next 3 runs that earn XP.

  • It only shows up in the last 7 days of a season.
  • It only shows up if you are too far behind to reach tier 30 at an even pace.
  • You can take one a day, and only when no boost is running.
  • It is opt in. You choose whether to watch.

Save your best runs for the boost. A long Endless March or Chaos run pays the most XP per run, so doubling it is worth the most.

Encore seasons

After Season 5 the clock keeps running, so every season stays timed. Season 6 is Encore 1 of Season 1, Season 7 is Encore 2 of Season 2, and so on around the list.

  • An encore reuses its base season's premium track. If you own that season's pass, the encore premium track is paid for you.
  • The free track is the same rewards in a new order. The order is fixed, so it is the same for every player. Tier 30 stays last.
  • Free tier 30 of an encore is a recolored headband. There is one color per encore, and the colors cycle every four encores.
  • Each encore takes a campaign rule in turn: Meteor Shower, Ice Field, Jungle Loot, then Neon Night.

This means a season you missed is not gone. Its premium looks come back on an encore, and a late purchase pays them all.

A simple War Pass plan

  • Claim your daily login, daily mission and trophy every day. That is 160 XP for very little effort.
  • Play the long modes when you want XP. Endless March, Gate Frenzy and Chaos add up to 20 XP for stages.
  • Beat bosses. Each one adds 5 XP, up to 20 XP per run.
  • Check the days left every few days. If you are behind in the last week, look for the boost.
  • Claim tiers as you go, so scrap reaches your Outpost builders sooner.
  • Decide on premium after you see the season's looks. You can buy it at any point and keep every tier you reached.

Questions

How much XP do I need for each War Pass tier in Gatecrash Army?

Each tier takes 400 XP and a season has 30 tiers, so tier 30 needs 12,000 XP. XP comes from finished runs, plus 40 for a claimed trophy, 60 for the daily login and 60 for a daily mission.

How long does a Gatecrash Army War Pass season last?

A season lasts 28 days. When the clock runs out, the game pays everything you can still claim and moves you to the next season. Your XP, tiers and claims reset, but extra bonus crate XP carries over.

Does the premium War Pass make my army stronger?

No. The premium track pays cosmetics only: hats, skins, tracers and a victory effect. It never pays coins, scrap or anything that changes a run. The free track pays coins, scrap and a few cosmetics.

What happens if I buy the premium pass late?

Buying premium unlocks every premium tier you have already reached in that season. If a season is already over, buying its pass later pays all of that season's premium rewards at once.
