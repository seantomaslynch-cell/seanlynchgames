LifeLine relationships, marriage and family guide

Relationships in LifeLine are not decoration. Who you date, whether you marry, how a split ends and who inherits your estate all change your cash, your stats and who carries your family forward. This guide covers the odds and costs behind each step, using US figures.

Dating and getting turned down

  • Find a date shows three adults, each with an age, looks, a job and a trait. You ask one out or pass on all three, and you get one ask a year. Closing the sheet shows the same three again.
  • The odds start at 55% for average looks. Looks move them up to 30 points either way. Net worth of 100,000 adds 4 points and 1,000,000 adds 8, while a negative net worth costs 5. Fame adds up to 10. Odds never go below 20% or above 90%.
  • You can make friends from age 5. Teens get a lighter version of dating with crushes, asking someone out and the school dance.
  • Time with people is capped at 3 interactions per person each year. The list includes cuddle, compliment, gift, deep talk, argue, spend time, insult and prank, and which ones show depends on your age and who it is. A gift costs 400.

Proposals, weddings and prenups

  • You can propose from age 18. The odds start at 2%, then add up to 45 points for relationship quality and up to 50 points for years together. Four years together is the full amount. The result stays between 3% and 95%.
  • A no costs 8 happiness, 2 reputation and 5 points of relationship quality. You normally get one proposal a year, and an ask-again option offers one more try.
  • A yes gives 10 happiness and 1 reputation. Then you plan the wedding. The seven venues are Courthouse at 200, Backyard at 3,000, Rustic Barn at 14,000, Beach Ceremony at 24,000, Vineyard at 55,000, Grand Ballroom at 110,000 and Clifftop Castle at 320,000. Happiness gains run from 6 to 20, and reputation from 0 to 15.
  • A prenup protects the net worth you had when you married. The catch is that above 400,000 in net worth, there is a 14% chance your fiancé is insulted and calls the wedding off. Marriage also brings in-laws into your People list.
  • Your spouse may ask you for money from age 18. Saying yes costs cash and warms the marriage. Saying no cools it.

Affairs and getting caught

  • Flirting needs a partner. The odds are your dating odds times 0.85, kept between 10% and 80%. There is a 15% chance someone tells your partner, which costs 6 to 12 relationship points and 2 reputation.
  • Romance with anyone other than your partner counts as an affair. You are caught 42% of the time, or 25% with protection, and always if you pick up an STI. Protection cuts the STI chance from 6% to 1% and cuts pregnancy to about an eighth.
  • If your spouse catches you, they divorce you for cause. You lose 65% to 82% of everything that counts toward net worth, owe alimony for 8 years at about 40% of your income (at least 9,000 a year), and lose 28 happiness and 14 reputation. A good lawyer offer that year cuts alimony to 4 years. A partner or fiancé simply leaves, and you get one talk to patch it up at 8% to 45% odds.

Divorce and alimony

  • Choosing to divorce splits half of everything net worth counts: cash, investments, business, home equity, cars and valuables. Moving money first protects nothing.
  • With a prenup, only what you built during the marriage is split, and there is no alimony. Without one, alimony runs 5 years at about 25% of your income (at least 6,000 a year).
  • A divorce costs 18 happiness and 3 health. If you have children, a custody case can open.

Having children

  • Trying for a baby starts at 18. Odds depend on who would carry: 60% up to 30, 36% at 35, 19% at 40, 5% at 45, under 1% at 50, and none after.
  • IVF is open from 21 to 55 and costs 18,000 a cycle. Odds are 55% through age 34, then fall 3.5 points a year, with a 5% floor. A failed cycle costs 7 happiness, and you can try again. Surrogacy costs 95,000 and works 82% of the time. Both work for singles and couples.
  • Adoption is open from 21 to 60 and costs 28,000. A quarter of that is charged for the application and home study even if you are turned down. Approval starts at 55%, gains 0.6 points for each point of reputation above 45, and gets 15 points for being married and 10 for net worth above 150,000. It stays between 20% and 95%.
  • Optional packs add depth. Parenthood lets you raise each child your way, with Strict, Easygoing, Hands-on and Hands-off styles. Custody and Family Court covers mediation, a parenting plan and support after a split. Hearts covers date nights and therapy to keep a marriage going.

Parents, siblings and elders

  • You can ask a parent for money from age 6, once a year per person. Nothing comes if your cash is over 200,000, and asking again within two years halves the odds. Asking for advice opens at 8.
  • At 18, if your cash is negative, one parent can pay up to 10,000 of what you owe. It happens once.
  • From age 16 you can care for a relative aged 65 or older. It costs 1,500 if you have it, and adds 6 to 12 relationship points and 3 happiness.
  • Making amends with a parent or sibling opens from age 10 when the relationship is under 45. It works 70% of the time.

When a life ends

  • From 18 you can write a will with a share for each person. With no will, the default estate share is 80% for a child or grandchild, 60% for a spouse, 45% for a sibling and 25% for a cousin.
  • The funeral comes out of the estate before anyone inherits. Pauper is free and costs 4 reputation. Modest tops out at 8,000 and adds 2. Grand tops out at 120,000 and adds 10. Small estates never pay more than 40% of their value.
  • You continue as a child, grandchild, spouse, sibling or cousin. The heir pays 20% estate tax on their share. A family lawyer offer cuts that to 10% once, and a Bloodline pack trust lowers it to 8%. The heir keeps traits and heirlooms, and a new job and spouse start fresh.

Questions people ask

Why did my partner turn down my proposal?

The odds start at 2%. Relationship quality adds up to 45 points and years together add up to 50, with four years the full amount. The result stays between 3% and 95%, so a short or shaky relationship often fails. A refusal costs 8 happiness, 2 reputation and 5 relationship points.

What do I lose in a LifeLine divorce?

A normal divorce splits half of everything net worth counts, and without a prenup you also owe alimony for 5 years. If you are caught cheating, you lose 65% to 82% and pay alimony for 8 years. A prenup limits the split to what you built during the marriage.

Can I have a baby later in life?

Yes. Trying naturally drops to 5% at 45 and ends after 50. IVF is open until 55 and costs 18,000, and surrogacy costs 95,000 at 82%. Adoption is open until 60 and costs 28,000.

How much estate tax do heirs pay?

The heir pays 20% on their share of the estate. A family lawyer offer cuts it to 10% once, and a Bloodline pack trust lowers it to 8%. Your will decides each share.
