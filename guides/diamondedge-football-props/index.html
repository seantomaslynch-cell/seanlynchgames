DiamondEdge NFL and college football props guide

DiamondEdge is an AI research app for player props, and football is one of its sports. This guide covers what the NFL and college football side actually shows: the markets, the role and matchup numbers, and where they sit in the app. For how the main rating works, read the AI Hit Chance guide. For splits and trends, see how to read matchups and trends. This page sticks to what is specific to football.

The football prop markets

  • Eight markets are covered for both the NFL and college football: passing yards, passing touchdowns, rushing yards, rushing touchdowns, receiving yards, receptions, rush plus receiving yards, and anytime touchdown.
  • On the board they appear as short chips: Pass Yds, Pass TDs, Rush Yds, Rec Yds, Receptions, Rush + Rec, Rush TDs and Anytime TD. Use the chips to filter the list to one market.
  • Anytime touchdown is a yes or no market, so it has no number line. The card shows "Anytime TD" where a line would normally be.
  • Football boards run by week, not by day. The weekly slate covers NFL weeks 1 to 18 and college regular-season weeks 1 to 16, plus the first two college postseason weeks.

How the chance is built

  • The football chance comes from the card's projection and the player's recent qualifying games. The NFL and college each have their own settings, fit on past seasons of data. Baseball and basketball formulas are not reused.
  • Alternate lines get their own chance. Passing yards ladders step in 25 and 50 yard rungs, and the other yardage markets step in 10, 20 and 30 yard rungs.
  • On whole-number lines, an exact tie is a push. The chance is adjusted for that instead of counting a tie as a miss.
  • College chances are more limited. College rows carry no position, so the model uses pooled settings. For college passing touchdowns, rushing touchdowns and anytime touchdown, the model did not pass its own accuracy check, so no chance is shown for them. The three yardage markets and receptions did pass.

Usage and role

  • Open a football prop and look for the Usage and role panel under More research. It is part of DiamondEdge Pro. Free viewers see a preview of the panel titles.
  • The table shows the player's share of the team: target share (or catch share where targets are missing), air yards share, average depth of target, carry share, and red zone and inside-10 shares for targets and carries. Quarterbacks do not get target or air yards share, because they throw the targets.
  • Each rate shows how many games sit behind it, with a recent window next to the season figure. Only games before this week count.
  • Team context adds pass rate over expected in close-game situations and team plays per game.
  • A teammate-out section compares the player's target and carry share without a key teammate against the games with him. It only appears when a teammate has enough share to matter.

The anytime touchdown estimate

  • The estimate starts from the player's share of the team's red zone chances and a touchdown rate that is pulled toward the league rate, so a hot two-game start does not read as a certainty. It needs at least 2 games.
  • It is scaled to the team's season scoring. You can type a team total to adjust it. You can also type American odds to see the break-even percentage next to the estimate.
  • Nothing you type is saved. DiamondEdge says it has no team total feed, so it does not invent one.

Matchup and game context

  • Defense vs position: the Form section of a card shows where this week's opponent ranks at allowing that stat to that position, using this season's games before the week. Rank 1 allows the most. A defense needs at least 2 games, and the rank is among 8 or more defenses.
  • On the board, only a soft matchup (top quarter) or a tough matchup (bottom quarter) gets a chip. The card sheet shows the full line for any rank. This is display only and does not change the chance.
  • NFL game environment: the venue's roof type, and for open-air U.S. stadiums the National Weather Service forecast at kickoff, with temperature, wind and chance of rain. Retractable roofs are labeled as a game-day decision.
  • College has no positions in its data, so the defense comparison covers the whole offense. Running backs are inferred from usage: a regular runner who is not a passer.

College-only context

  • The college section can show a blowout-risk note from the spread, plays per game for both teams, SP+ ratings and a share of team plays with blowout time removed.
  • Plain notes flag the quirks. Sacks count as rushing plays in college, so a quarterback's rushing yards include sack losses. There is also no NCAA-wide injury report, so availability can be incomplete.

Where to find it

  • Use the sport switcher on the board to pick NFL or college football.
  • Open any prop to see the card sheet. Alternate lines are in their own group on it.
  • The football schedule view lists games for today and the next two days, with kickoff times in your time zone. Tap a team to open its player research. A college kickoff at exactly midnight Eastern is shown as TBD.
  • On the NFL board, Pro members also see projected scores: DiamondEdge's own score for each game next to the score implied by the lines, with offense and defense ranks. It is research context, not a pick.

Common questions

Which football prop markets does DiamondEdge cover?

Passing yards, passing touchdowns, rushing yards, rushing touchdowns, receiving yards, receptions, rush plus receiving yards and anytime touchdown, for both the NFL and college football.

Why do some college football props show no chance?

For college passing touchdowns, rushing touchdowns and anytime touchdown, the chance model did not pass its own accuracy check, so DiamondEdge does not show a chance there. The three yardage markets and receptions did pass.

Does DiamondEdge show a weather forecast for football games?

For NFL games at open-air U.S. stadiums, the card can show the kickoff forecast from the National Weather Service: temperature, wind and chance of rain. Dome stadiums are marked as domes, and retractable-roof stadiums say the roof call is made on game day.

Are the usage and role stats free?

The usage and role panel, with target and carry shares and the anytime touchdown estimate, is part of DiamondEdge Pro. Free viewers see a preview of the panel titles.
