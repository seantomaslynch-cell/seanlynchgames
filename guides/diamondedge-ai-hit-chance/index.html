What is AI Hit Chance? How DiamondEdge rates player props

DiamondEdge is an AI research app for player props in MLB, the NBA, the NFL and college football. It is a research tool, not a sportsbook. Here is what the main numbers mean.

App StoreGoogle Play

AI Hit Chance

  • Every prop gets a percentage: the model's estimate of how often the player clears the line.
  • Those chances are checked against how past picks at the same chance actually finished.

What goes into it

  • The model scores props every day using recent form, the matchup, workload and role, park and weather, and lineup status.
  • Each prop also gets an AI projection against the line and a plain-English write-up of why it stands out and the risks to watch.

Where to look

  • Today's board: every scored prop, with last 5 and last 10 hit rates and game-by-game charts.
  • Prime Picks and Top Picks: the day's highest-rated props.
  • Lines and alt lines: the chance at the main line and at each alternate rung.
  • Trends: hot streaks, 8-of-10 hitters and cold spells.

Results in public

  • Picks are graded in public after every game, misses included.
  • Save props and see how they graded later.

Pro and the fine print

  • Pro unlocks every AI Hit Chance, projection and write-up, plus filters, player pages and unlimited saves. The yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers.
  • DiamondEdge does not accept wagers and shows no odds. No outcome is guaranteed. You must be 21 or older. If gambling stops being fun, call 1-800-GAMBLER.

Get DiamondEdge

DiamondEdge is free on iPhone and Android. See the DiamondEdge page, the latest updates, or ask questions in the Discord.
