Gatecrash Army Outpost and expeditions: what to build first

Gatecrash Army has a base between runs called the Outpost. It opens at level 4 and runs on scrap, a currency you earn by playing, plus build timers. This guide covers what each building does, how builders and timers work, how to earn scrap faster, and how expeditions pay out. For gates, officers and bosses, see the Gatecrash Army tips and the upgrades and bosses guide.

The ten buildings

  • Command Tent: adds 2% coins per level and caps every other building. It goes to level 15. At level 11 it also lets you trade scrap for coins.
  • Barracks: +1 starting soldier per level above 1, so level 10 gives +9.
  • Armory: +3% fire rate and damage per level above 1, up to +27%.
  • Workshop: weapon crates last 1 second longer per level above 1.
  • Field Hospital: a free revive every day with no video, 1 at level 3, 2 at level 6 and 3 at level 9.
  • Radio Tower: earns coins while you are away. Come back and collect.
  • Supply Depot: the free chest fills faster, from every 4 hours at level 1 to every 2.2 hours at level 10, and it pays 10% more coins per level above 1.
  • Watchtower (Command Tent 3): a shield covers your squad at the start of every run, 0.5 seconds per level above 1. From level 6 you also open with an airstrike.
  • Trophy Hall (Command Tent 4): +5% scrap per level above 1.
  • Training Ground (Command Tent 5): officer rank-ups cost 3% fewer coins per level above 1, 27% less at level 10.

Costs, timers and builders

  • Every building starts at level 1 and tops out at level 10, except the Command Tent at 15.
  • An upgrade costs scrap that rises 50% each level. The first upgrade costs 8 for Barracks, 9 for Radio Tower, 10 for Command Tent, Workshop and Trophy Hall, 11 for Supply Depot, 12 for Armory and Watchtower, 13 for Training Ground and 14 for Field Hospital.
  • Timers grow fast. Level 1 to 2 takes about 20 seconds, then 2 minutes, 8, 20, 45 and 90 minutes, then 3, 5 and 8 hours. Levels 11 to 15 on the Command Tent take 12, 15, 18, 21 and 24 hours and cost 600, 900, 1,300, 1,800 and 2,500 scrap.
  • No other building can pass the Command Tent's level, so raise the Tent when something shows as blocked.
  • You start with one builder. A second joins when the Command Tent hits level 5, and a third comes from a 7 day login streak. Each builder works on one upgrade at a time.
  • A rewarded video can finish a running timer. Builders and money never speed one up.

Earning scrap faster

Scrap comes from runs. A campaign win pays 6 plus your level, so a level 20 win pays 26. A loss still pays 2 plus 1 for every 4 levels, so a bad night still builds something. Boss Rush pays 4 scrap per boss. Endless March, Gate Frenzy and Chaos pay 3 per stage you reach.

The Trophy Hall multiplies all of this, and expeditions add a second stream that needs no play time at all.

Radio coins, the free chest and scrap trades

  • The Radio Tower pays 25 coins an hour per level and stores 4 hours plus half an hour per level. At level 1 that is a pile of about 112 coins, and at level 10 it is 250 an hour and a 9 hour pile of 2,250. Collect before it fills, since a full tower stops earning.
  • The Supply Depot makes your free chest come back sooner and pay more, so level it if you open the chest often.
  • From Command Tent 11 you can trade 200 scrap for coins, up to 3 times a day. The payout is 100 coins times your Tent level minus 9, so 200 coins at level 11 and 600 at level 15.
  • Camp milestones: every 10 building levels you gain across the camp unlocks a chest. The first pays 100 coins and 10 scrap (plus 50 coins and 10 scrap more for each later chest).

Expeditions

Send one scout team at a time. The timer runs in real time, so it keeps going while the game is closed. Scrap is boosted 8% per Command Tent level above 1.

  • Patrol: 30 minutes, 25 scrap, 10% shard chance.
  • Scout Run: 1 hour, 45 scrap, 18%.
  • Salvage Trip: 2 hours, 80 scrap, 30%.
  • Supply Raid: 4 hours, 140 scrap, 50%.
  • Deep Recon: 8 hours, 240 scrap, 80%.
  • Expeditions of 4 hours or more have a 50% chance to give 2 shards instead of 1 when they find any.
  • Collect 10 shards to redeem one random look you do not own yet. The pool is only skins, tracers, hats and victory poses that you can earn for free, never paid or prestige looks.
  • A rewarded video can bring the team back early. It is limited each day, so it is best saved for a long trip.

Camp styles

Styles only change how the camp looks. Field Camp is free. Beach, Desert, Frost, Lava and Neon camps unlock as you reach worlds 1 to 5, one world for every 5 levels you clear. Three more looks come in the Base Pack. Nothing here changes your squad's strength.

A simple build order

  • Start by upgrading the Command Tent, Barracks and Armory, since they help every run.
  • Build the Trophy Hall as soon as Command Tent 4 opens it, so every later upgrade comes quicker.
  • Use the Field Hospital for a free revive each day and the Watchtower for safer starts.
  • Keep your builders busy before you close the game, and start an expedition at the same time.

Questions

What do I build first in the Gatecrash Army Outpost?

Raise the Command Tent early, because it caps every other building and unlocks the Watchtower (Command Tent 3), Trophy Hall (Command Tent 4) and Training Ground (Command Tent 5). Then put scrap into Barracks for extra starting soldiers and Armory for fire rate and damage.

How do I get scrap in Gatecrash Army?

Scrap pays out after every run. A campaign win pays 6 plus your level, and a loss pays 2 plus one for every 4 levels. Boss Rush pays 4 per boss, and Endless March, Gate Frenzy and Chaos pay 3 per stage. Expeditions and the scrap bonus from the Trophy Hall add more.

How do expedition shards work?

Each finished expedition has a chance to find shards, from 10% on a Patrol up to 80% on Deep Recon. Collect 10 shards to redeem a random look you do not own yet, chosen only from free to earn skins, tracers, hats and victory poses.

Can I pay to make the Outpost stronger?

No. Buildings cost scrap and time. A rewarded video can finish a build timer or bring an expedition back early, but money does not buy upgrades. The only paid Outpost item is the Base Pack of camp looks, which change appearance only.
