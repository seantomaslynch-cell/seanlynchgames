Gridiron GM beginner's guide: run a pro football team

Gridiron GM is a free football general manager sim for iPhone and Android. You build the roster, decide who gets paid and make the calls that swing a season, while an owner watches the standings.

App StoreGoogle Play

Respect the cap

  • Signing bonuses, proration, dead money, restructures and the franchise tag all work the way you would expect.
  • Cut a star at the wrong time and the dead money stays with you for years.
  • Free agency is a market: rivals bid against you and a lowball offer gets a no.

Trade with a plan

  • AI general managers value players the way a front office would.
  • They tell you how far apart you are, so you can adjust an offer instead of guessing.

Scout before you draft

  • Every prospect is a range, not a number.
  • The range only narrows when you spend scouting time on that player.
  • Use mock drafts and the live draft room. Late-round gems go to the teams that did their homework.

Game day

  • Pick how to play each game: Key moments, Play-by-play or Quick sim.
  • Fourth downs, two-point tries and late timeouts are your call, or let your coach decide.
  • Win and your stadium fills up. Prefer the front office only? Turn field view off in Settings.

Pace your career

  • Sim to the trade deadline, the end of the season or through a playoff bye. The game stops when something needs you.
  • Three save slots, offline play and no account.
  • Get fired and teams that need a rebuild will call. Packs never raise a rating or give you an edge.

Get Gridiron GM

Gridiron GM is free on iPhone and Android. See the Gridiron GM page, the latest updates, or ask questions in the Discord.
