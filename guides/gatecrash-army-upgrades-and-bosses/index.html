Gatecrash Army upgrades, gates and bosses explained

Gatecrash Army is a free action runner for iPhone and Android. This guide goes deeper than the basics: what each upgrade does, how every gate behaves, which perks and officers to pick, and how to read a boss fight. New here? Start with the starter tips.

App StoreGoogle Play

The three coin upgrades

  • There are three permanent upgrades: Start squad, Fire rate and Bullet power. Each goes up to level 25.
  • Start squad gives you 1 soldier plus 1 per level, so level 25 starts a run with 26.
  • Fire rate starts at 2.5 shots per second and adds 10% of that base for each level.
  • Bullet power starts at 1.0 damage and adds 0.2 per level.
  • The first level costs 40 coins for Start squad and 50 each for the other two. Every level costs more than the last.
  • The same coins also pay for officers, so do not spend them all in one place.

Gate types

  • Blue plus gates and multiply gates add to your squad. Red minus and divide gates take soldiers away.
  • Plus and minus gates can be shot. A bar on the gate fills as you hit it, and the number climbs. Gates need a few more hits at higher levels.
  • A purple ? gate hides its result until you cross it. Most hidden results are good.
  • From level 6, power gates appear opposite a soldier gate. Green Fire Rate gives +20% fire rate and orange Power gives +25% damage for the rest of the run. Pick men or power.
  • Special gates give a weapon for 10 seconds, bonus coins, or No Losses, which stops you losing soldiers for 4 seconds.
  • Most levels from level 3 have one golden gate near the middle. It is a rewarded ad that doubles your squad, and it is optional.

Perks: pick well, keep them

  • After a campaign win you pick 1 of 3 perks. A kind can be taken twice, and you can hold 6 in total.
  • The pool includes Recruiter (+3 soldiers at the start), Gate Hacker (plus gates give +2 more), Trigger Happy (+12% fire rate), Hollow Points (+15% bullet damage), Looter (+20% coins) and Giant Killer (+25% damage to bosses).
  • Quartermaster makes weapon crates last 5 seconds longer, and Field Medic adds a soldier every 5 seconds.
  • Second Wind opens at level 12 and returns 5 soldiers after your first wipe. Gate Gunner opens at level 20 and fills shootable gates 30% faster.
  • From level 4, a normal level pauses once partway through and offers a perk draft. A drafted perk lasts for that run only.
  • Losing a level clears your perks, so protect a win streak.

Officers and ranks

  • You bring one officer per run. Each has one ability on a cooldown button.
  • Scout is free and calls in extra soldiers every 35 seconds.
  • Gunsmith costs 800 coins and drops a random weapon crate (40 second cooldown). Demolitions costs 1,500 and calls an airstrike (45 seconds). Prospector costs 2,500 and doubles coins for 8 seconds (40 seconds).
  • Guardian blocks all soldier losses for 4 seconds (55 second cooldown). Juggernaut boosts fire rate and damage for 6 seconds (50 second cooldown). Both come from lifetime trophies, not coins.
  • Officers rank up from 1 to 10 with coins only. Each rank cuts the cooldown 3% and adds 5% to the effect. The first rank costs 2,000 coins.
  • Keep Guardian or Shield Wall for a boss move you cannot dodge.

Enemies that change the fight

  • Bombers appear from level 14. They run at your squad and blow up on contact, taking out up to 3 soldiers. Shoot them early.
  • Splitters appear from level 18 and break into 2 small grunts when they die.
  • Chargers appear from level 21. They stop for a short wind-up, then dash in a straight line, so sidestep.
  • Healers appear from level 24 and heal nearby enemies every 2.6 seconds. They pay the most coins, so kill them first.
  • Titans appear from level 121. They are slow, armored and trample 5 soldiers before they fall.
  • In normal and challenge runs your squad evolves. At 60, 130 and 210 soldiers it becomes Veterans, Elite and Legends, each adding 10% damage.

Reading the boss

  • Every boss move is called out about 0.7 seconds before it lands. Use that gap to move.
  • After each move ends, the boss is stunned and takes double damage for 2 seconds. Dodge, then fire.
  • Below half health the boss becomes enraged and its moves come faster.
  • Stomp hits soldiers near the front of the squad, so spread out. Volley fires arrows in a fan, charge is a straight rush, and summon calls in backup.
  • From level 6 each boss gets a third move with a visible safe spot, such as a shockwave, a laser sweep or a wall. Step into the safe area.
  • Giant Killer and a strong Fire rate upgrade help here, because the stun window rewards big bursts.

Questions

Which upgrade should I buy first?

All three coin upgrades max at level 25. The first level of Start squad costs 40 coins, and the first level of Fire rate and Bullet power costs 50 each. Costs rise with every level, so spreading coins across all three keeps each purchase cheap.

Can I buy power with real money?

No. The store sells ad removal, looks and mode packs. Officers are earned with coins or a trophy, and perks are earned by winning.

Why did I lose my perks?

Perks last until your squad loses a level. A rewarded ad can keep them after a loss.

How do I survive a boss stomp?

Spread your squad out when STOMP! flashes. The stomp only hits soldiers near the front of the squad, so a spread squad loses fewer.
